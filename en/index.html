Grow together, not alone.

Nedabah Way is a faith community on Jeju Island, Korea. "Nedabah" is Hebrew for a freewill offering — a path begun by choice, not pressure. We help people rebuild direction and rhythm through gatherings, notes, education, and AI tools. Several programs run online and in English on request.

FAQ

What is Nedabah Way?

A nonprofit faith community in Seogwipo, Jeju, Korea. Gatherings, publications, education, and AI/automation — to rebuild direction and rhythm, together.

Available in English or online?

Notes, coaching, AI labs, and automation consults can run online; English on request. Email us to ask.

Is it free to join?

Weekly gatherings and web articles are free. We run on voluntary support; some institutional workshops are arranged separately.
