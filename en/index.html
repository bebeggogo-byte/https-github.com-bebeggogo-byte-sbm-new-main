Nedabah Way: a nonprofit education group in Jeju, Korea

Nedabah Way is an unincorporated nonprofit education group based in Seogwipo, Jeju Island, Korea. It is registered as an education-service business and opened on 28 March 2025. Nedabah is a Hebrew word meaning a freewill offering. The group runs workshops, coaching and AI labs for schools, institutions and young adults, and publishes notes. Several programs run online, and English is available on request.

AURATO career consulting

Career consulting is run under the AURATO brand. It is 1:1 online consulting for people applying to hospital jobs, including nurses, clinical laboratory technologists, radiologic technologists and physical therapists. Details are on the AURATO career consulting page (in Korean).

Notes

Nedabah Way publishes short articles on learning, perspective, habits, AI and automation. The articles cite sources including Havruta and the Talmud, Atomic Habits, Building a Second Brain and Daniel Kahneman. The notes archive lists all articles, and an RSS feed is available.

FAQ

What is Nedabah Way?

An unincorporated nonprofit education group based in Seogwipo, Jeju Island, Korea. It runs workshops, coaching, and AI and automation programs, and publishes notes.

Does Nedabah Way offer career consulting?

Yes. Career consulting is run under the AURATO brand. Details are on the AURATO career consulting page (in Korean).

Are programs available in English or online?

Notes, coaching, AI labs and automation consultations can run online. English is available on request by email at nedabah.way@gmail.com.

Is it free to join?

The web articles are free. Nedabah Way runs on voluntary support and partnerships. Fees for some institutional workshops are arranged separately.
